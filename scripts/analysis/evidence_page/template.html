The search procedure and the evidence for each choice

What the study does to turn a labelled training set of D sequences into a student ensemble, with every decision followed by the measurements that support it, and everything tried and left out. Written Nov 3, 2026 from the stored results; sources are repository files named under each figure. Source write-up: docs/SEARCH_PROCEDURE_AND_EVIDENCE.md.

Updated Nov 3, afternoon. The question was whether non-LegNet designs lose because they are weaker or because they were never trained for 200–300 epochs. The stored runs and a partial bake-off both point at the recipe: see Epochs and diversity. It also records a bug in two of my arms (ar40, ia40 trained 80 epochs, not 300), now fixed and relaunched as ar41 and ln41.

The procedure at a glance

0 · DataNested pools or independent replicate pools; oracle_v3 labels; V0c (chr19/21/X) for selection.
1 · Input standard240 bp = insert + 20 real flank bases each side; shift ±10; reverse-complement augmentation; two-strand average at test.
2 · Bank (no LLM)Published MPRA-LegNet, per-size recipe, 20 seeds, no early stopping.10k e800 wd 0.4 · 30k e500 wd 0.2 · 100k e500 wd 0.1
3 · Loop (LLM)15 rounds × 2 models: propose 6 → coordinate 2 → code → train in harness H3.Sonnet 5.5, V0c feedback, explore 40% then exploit
4 · EnsemblePositive ElasticNetCV on z-scored validation predictions of everything trained.

Decisions and the evidence for each

Grades follow the Oct 30 audit: A paired, several pools or architectures, effect clear of noise · B one pool or size, or inside the noise · C assumed or never tested at that size. Seed noise: SE 0.0013 for one model; a loop run has SD 0.006–0.008 on the stack.

1

Train, select and score on oracle_v3 labels; report experimental labels as a second column

A

Students distil the oracle, so their ordering and gaps are the same against experimental labels but shifted down by about 0.04.

Oracle against experiment r = 0.931 on held-out sequences. The gap between label sets is 0.038–0.043 at every size.

Limit Students distil the oracle, which flatters anything that resembles its backbone, so AlphaGenome-based students are baselines only.

Sources docs/TUNED_LEGNET.md, docs/per_d_procedure_audit_oct30.md

2

Nested pools for size comparisons; independent replicate pools for every comparison between methods

A

Three independent pools beat three seeds on one pool in 12 of 12 comparisons.

Search-trajectory luck and pool luck are both real: across three pools a loop stack varies by SD 0.0075 (rr39) and 0.0015 (rr40). The Oct 13–14 scaling cells used a separate pool per size, which made the genomic curve flat from 30k to 100k.

Sources docs/per_d_procedure_audit_oct30.md, scripts/analysis/compare_legnet_vs_loop.py

3

Input: 20 real flank bases per side, shift ±10, reverse-complement

A

Real plasmid sequence around the insert is worth about +0.07 test r. More context adds nothing, and the oracle's own augmentation does not help the students.

Context saturates at 12–20 bases (+0.069 to +0.075). 200 bases is −0.007 against 20; no shift is −0.012; ±30 is −0.015. Reverse-complement averaging at test time adds +0.014. A sweep at 10k and 100k and a fairness check on ten reservoirs found the same plateau and a rank correlation of 0.86–0.99 with and without context.

Not tested The oracle-style variants were tested only on the menu members; the oracle trains on about 800k sequences and can afford weaker augmentation.

Sources docs/STUDY_LOG_OCT29_NOV2.md, docs/MENU.md, scripts/program_oct22/register_fx35b.py

4

The bank's recipe per size: long AdamW one-cycle schedule, no early stopping

A

The published recipe (100 epochs) is under-trained at these sizes. Smaller data wants more epochs and more weight decay; stopping inside a one-cycle schedule is far worse than running it out.

Tuned over published: +0.066 (10k, SE 0.005), +0.024 (30k, SE 0.001), +0.007 (100k). At 30k 200 epochs cost −0.006 and 300 epochs are within 0.002 of 500; weight decay 0.4 is too strong at 30k, and 0.2 hurts at 100k. A one-cycle schedule only anneals in its last third, so the best epoch is always near the end (473 of 500 on average at 30k).

Limits At 10k the gain was still rising at 800 epochs. 300k and larger sizes are not tuned. The chosen arm came from the same sweep it is reported on (margins are 5–10 SE).

Sources docs/TUNED_LEGNET.md, scripts/program_oct22/bench33_legnet.py, lr38_* cells

5

Loop harness H3: patience that never cuts a one-cycle schedule, fp16, single-pass reverse-complement augmentation

A

The loop launcher stopped every model after 8 stagnant epochs, and its reverse-complement consistency loss cost the same LegNet 0.032. Fixing both took the loop from below the bank to within 0.004 of it.

The patience fix is +0.021 on the stack, the single-pass reverse-complement augmentation another +0.012; the best single model of every rr40 and lv40 run is 0.871–0.878 (bar 0.86, bank mean single 0.875). Loop plus bank beats the bank for the first time: 0.8975 against 0.8907.

Limit The cause of any remaining gap at 10k and 100k is unknown until those arms finish.

12

15 rounds × 2 models; 6 ideas per round

A

Returns fall steeply after the first exploit rounds; more than about five ideas per call adds nothing.

The share of rounds with a new best falls from 67% (round 1) to under 30% after round 5 and to near 0–10% late. Rounds 10 to 15 add +0.002 on the stack; 50-round runs (203 models) are no better than 30-model runs. Ten rounds of six proposals beat 60 serial rounds by +0.016, and two proposals per round over 30 rounds was the best cadence.

Running rl40: 30 rounds under H3.

V0c (genomic, chr19/21/X) for stopping, selection, proposer feedback and the stack

A

Validation r ranks the models of a run almost as test r does for genomic-like sets; the selection signal for OOD and the GC sweep is weak.

The validation winners sit +0.0023 (SE 0.0006) above the line fitted on the other models: no winner's curse. Regret of taking the validation winner: 0.002 on genomic, 0.019 on OOD, 0.025 on the GC sweep. 15

Positive ElasticNetCV stack over everything trained

A

Weights fitted on validation predictions, members with weight zero unused. It ties greedy selection and beats the plain mean.

The stack beats the plain mean by 0.002–0.018 over 12 cells. Greedy K = 5 is 0.01 lower. Uncertainty weighting and diversity-aware (DPP) selection give 0.0000. Dropping the bottom members costs at most 0.0001 when chosen by retrained validation r, because the positive stack already zero-weights them.

The frozen menu is used only at 10k

A

Epochs and diversity: do non-LegNet designs lose because they train too briefly?

Measured on the finished standard 30k loop runs plus a recipe-matched bake-off that is still running. Stage counts (proposed, picked) use all 21 runs; figures of validation r use the 18 runs validated on V0c (540 models; the mixed-validation arm has a different validation set). Genomic test r is reported but never used to choose anything.

The loop gave LegNet descendants the long schedules and everything else the short ones

Only 1 of 113 independent designs ever trained for 200 epochs or more, against 209 of 427 LegNet-named designs. LegNet-named models trained under 100 epochs average 0.840 validation r, against 0.882 for those trained 200 or more.

Independent designs trained a median of 58 epochs with AdamW batch 128 or Muon batch 256; LegNet-named ones a median of 150 with AdamW batch 512–1024. At equal short training (under 100 epochs) the gap is 0.045 (0.840 against 0.795); at 300 epochs in the bake-off most independent designs reach 0.89–0.90 validation r (partial; see the table below), against 0.905–0.909 for the plain LegNet variants.

The earlier tests with longer schedules

Three earlier experiments looked at training longer or with the LegNet recipe. They varied different things, which is why they disagree.

Menu members at 3× epochs, patience off (their own Muon recipe, 60–80 → 180–240 epochs): no change, so the menu is not under-trained on its own recipe. LegNet's 100-epoch recipe on menu and the collaborator's members: −0.02 to −0.07, but 100 epochs is itself under-trained at 30k (+0.024 from the tuned recipe). Bake-off, long AdamW one-cycle: the first test that gives non-LegNet designs the combination LegNet needs. The step counts are similar (bs 256 × 70 epochs ≈ 8.2k steps; bs 1024 × 300 ≈ 8.8k), so the gain in the bake-off is not simply more updates: the large-batch AdamW recipe with weight decay and many augmented passes is what changes.

Sources ep38_*, tr38_*, docs/TUNED_LEGNET.md

The funnel: proposed, picked, trained, weighted, refined

Designs that do not name the LegNet trunk are proposed in the explore phase and picked at the same rate as the others. They are lost afterwards.

The coordinator is neutral: 35% against 32% in the explore rounds, 30% against 33% in the exploit rounds. The exploit incumbent is always the global best, which is a LegNet descendant, so 91% of exploit proposals build on it and the best independent design's best-so-far validation r is unchanged from round 5 to the end of the run in 19 of 21 runs. Diversity that reaches the stack is a BiGRU, attention or U-Net part on the LegNet trunk (16%, 9% and 3% of the weight); independent trunks hold 1%.

Caveat "Independent" is judged from the design text (no mention of LegNet or EffBlock); no such model contains EffBlock in its code, but token similarity to the reference is not informative (median 0.13), so a text flag is the best available.

Sources docs/DIVERSITY_FUNNEL_NOV3.md, data_diversity_funnel_30k.json

What would add evidence, in order

  1. Does an independent family reach rough optimality when trained like LegNet? running bake-off, ar41, ln41, is40. Rule: within 0.01 validation r of the best LegNet-derived member under the matched recipe, and useful if adding it raises the stack by more than 0.003.
  2. The loop at 10k and 100k under H3. running / waiting for H100.
  3. Explore fraction and mixed validation under H3 on more pools. Both current results are three pools under H2.
  4. Loss function. Everything is MSE although the score is Pearson r; Huber, correlation and rank losses were never compared. not started
  5. Give the proposer the ensemble signal (stack weights and member correlation; today only the coordinator sees them). not started
  6. Several strong, different references in the prompt, once the collaborator's best non-LegNet designs are retrained at their own best. not started
  7. Equal-compute comparison of "search per size and keep the best trained models" against a frozen menu. not started
  8. Recipe sweeps for non-LegNet families at each size. The bake-off is the first step at 30k.
  9. A noise ceiling from replicate-replicate r of the experimental data, and the SNV-effect encoder fix (indels are left-aligned in loop and menu encoders, centre-padded in the bank).
  10. 300k and larger sizes are untuned for both the bank and the loop.
